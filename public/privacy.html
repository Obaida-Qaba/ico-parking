Privacy

Portway is an independent ICO parking finder. Your browser asks for permission before sharing your device location with the app.

Your location is used to show your position, choose nearby terminals and calculate a route on your device. Saved parking points and recent searches are stored in your browser. Destination share links contain the selected parking and optional line, not your live location or saved GPS point.

Google satellite maps

Google receives map requests for the area shown on screen. When the map follows your location, that area reflects your position. Google may also receive your IP address and browser or device information as part of providing maps. Google handles this information under its Privacy Policy. Satellite photos were captured earlier and do not show live activity.

Offline use and hosting

The app stores its own plans, destination catalogue and routing data for offline use. It does not download Google satellite imagery for offline storage. Google satellite maps need a connection; the local road map remains available offline. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Clear this site's browser storage to remove saved points and recent searches. You can withdraw location permission in your browser settings.
