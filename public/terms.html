Using Portway

Portway is an independent parking finder, not an official ICO navigation service. Source plans and imagery may show an older layout. Follow current terminal signs, closures, access permissions and staff instructions.

A route may end at a parking approach or a shared area entrance, as labelled in the app. A parking code or line number does not establish a surveyed entrance or exact bay position. Stop safely before interacting with the map.

Google Maps features are subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Terms of Service. Read our privacy information for how the app uses your location and local storage.

Plans: ICO / Cewez. Independent routing data: OpenStreetMap contributors. Google supplies the satellite imagery; Portway's own catalogue and road data supply its parking labels and routes.
